How we work

Our creative process

Four clear steps from brief to delivery. No fluff, no back-and-forths just clean, creative execution.

01
Discovery
We start with a deep-dive into your brand, goals and audience. Understanding the why before touching the timeline.
02
Concept
Mood boards, style references and a creative direction deck. We align on the vision before a single frame is cut.
03
Production
Edit, colour, sound design and motion all done in-house. Progress previews and two rounds of revisions, included.
04
Delivery
Final files optimised for every platform, plus a debrief to set up your next project before this one even launches.

Who we are

A studio built on
craft and clarity

PixelBloom started as one editor's obsession with the perfect cut. Today it's a tight-knit creative studio serving creators, brands and couples who care deeply about how their story looks on screen.

We believe great video isn't just technically sharp it's emotionally resonant. Every project we take on gets our full creative energy, from concept to final export.
